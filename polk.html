James Knox Polk

James Knox Polk was the 11th president of the United States, serving from 1845 to 1849.

He previously was the 13th Speaker of the House of Representatives and ninth governor of Tennessee.

Polk was a member of the Democratic Party and an advocate of Jacksonian democracy.

James K. Polk

James Knox Polk served as the 11th President of the United States from 1845 to 1849.

Polk was known for his strong leadership and his role in expanding the territory of the United States during his presidency.

Before becoming president, Polk served as Speaker of the House of Representatives and as governor of Tennessee.

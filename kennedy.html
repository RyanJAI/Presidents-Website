John F. Kennedy

John F. Kennedy was the 35th President of the United States, serving from 1961 until 1963.

Kennedy was the youngest man elected to the presidency. His administration dealt with several major events, including the Cuban Missile Crisis and the beginning of the space race.

On November 22, 1963, Kennedy was assassinated in Dallas, Texas. He became the youngest President to die while in office.

President Kennedy was followed by Lyndon B. Johnson.
